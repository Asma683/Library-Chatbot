## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative yet inviting digital environment tailored for higher education scholarship, university library research, and contextual academic inquiry. The identity balances the institutional prestige of a historic research university with the frictionless precision of an advanced generative AI assistant. 

The aesthetic is Modern Scholarly Minimalism with tactile depth:
- **Atmosphere:** Rigorous, reliable, tranquil, and scholarly. The interface recedes into the background, prioritizing complex citations, full-text reading, and conversational dialogue.
- **Visual Tenets:** Generous whitespace, structural alignment, subtle glass layers for persistent navigation, crisp micro-borders, and high-legibility typographic hierarchies.
- **Target Audience:** University students, doctoral researchers, faculty members, and research librarians requiring trustworthy synthesis, academic citation transparency, and focus-driven study workflows.

## Colors

The color palette anchors on deep institutional navy, energetic royal indigo accents, and tranquil neutral grays.

- **Primary (`#102342` - Oxford Navy):** Represents institutional credibility, authority, and grounding. Applied to primary headings, user query message bubbles, prominent CTAs, and active navigation nodes.
- **Secondary (`#2563EB` - Royal Scholar Blue):** Represents computational intelligence and clarity. Used for conversational highlights, citation badges, action triggers, text links, and focus rings.
- **Tertiary (`#0D9488` - Academic Teal):** Used for verified source indicators, peer-reviewed collection badges, and completed state cues.
- **Neutral Palette:**
  - Canvas background: `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) creates a calm, glare-free working plane.
  - Surface cards & Assistant message bubbles: `#FFFFFF` (Crisp White).
  - Sidebar & Secondary surfaces: `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100).
  - Borders & Dividers: `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200).
  - Body text: `#1E293B` (Slate 800) for contrast and reading endurance.
  - Muted metadata & timestamps: `#64748B` (Slate 500).

## Typography

The typographic pairing bridges forward-thinking clarity with scholarly precision:
- **Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for its contemporary geometry and confident presence. It gives prompts, headers, and section leads a modern technological voice without feeling detached or clinical.
- **Body & Labels (Inter):** Highly legible across long-form synthesis, citation excerpts, abstract snippets, and dense table views. The tall x-height minimizes fatigue during research sessions.
- **Code & Citations (JetBrains Mono):** Monospaced figures and code snippets use JetBrains Mono for DOI links, Call Numbers, ISBN strings, and data syntax.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout balances a persistent reference pane with an uncluttered central conversational workspace:

- **Desktop Structure:** A fixed-width collateral sidebar (`280px`) houses catalog shortcuts, active research threads, and pinned bibliographies. The conversational thread is centered within a maximum read width of `860px` to maintain optimal line lengths.
- **Tablet Structure:** Sidebar collapses into an overlay drawer or a slim icon bar (`64px`). Margins contract to `1.5rem`.
- **Mobile Structure:** Single-column stacked fluid layout with full-width conversational stream, sticky top app bar, and anchored bottom prompt bar.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** 4px baseline sub-grid with consistent component gaps defined through strict token assignments (`space-xs` through `space-xl`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined hybrid of tonal staging, micro-borders, and diffused ambient shadows:

- **Tonal Layers:** The canvas rests at `#F8FAFC`. Chat surfaces, answer blocks, and cards elevate onto `#FFFFFF` equipped with a hairline border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`).
- **Ambient Shadows:**
  - *Resting Cards / AI Responses:* `0 1px 3px rgba(16, 35, 66, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(16, 35, 66, 0.02)`.
  - *Active Popovers / Citation Previews / Dropdowns:* `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(16, 35, 66, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(16, 35, 66, 0.04)`.
  - *Floating Composer Box:* `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(16, 35, 66, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(16, 35, 66, 0.03)`.
- **Glassmorphic Accents:** Sticky header bars and floating bottom composer backdrops apply `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over `rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.85)` with a subtle bottom divider.

## Shapes

The design system implements a refined rounded aesthetic (`roundedness: 2` / base `8px`), lending a polished and contemporary feel while preserving structural seriousness:

- **Micro Components (Chips, Badges, Citations):** `6px` to `8px` corner radius.
- **Input Fields & Buttons:** `8px` to `10px` for structured, comfortable click targets.
- **Assistant Response Containers & Cards:** `12px` to `16px` (`rounded-lg`).
- **Conversational User Bubbles:** Asymmetric rounding with `16px` overall and a reduced `4px` radius at the bottom-right anchor.
- **Search & Floating Prompt Bar:** `20px` to `24px` for an approachable, natural conversational input.

## Components

- **Conversational Bubbles:**
  - *User Bubble:* Right-aligned, `#102342` solid background, `#FFFFFF` text, `16px` padding, asymmetric rounding (`16px 16px 4px 16px`).
  - *AI Assistant Bubble:* Left-aligned, `#FFFFFF` crisp card, `1px solid #E2E8F0` border, `20px` padding, `16px` uniform rounding, paired with a small institutional crest/bot mark.
- **Input Composer (Chat Prompt Bar):**
  - Floating container with full-width interior field, pill-inspired contour (`24px`), soft elevation shadow, quick-action attachment and source filter icons, accompanied by a circular navy submit button with royal blue hover transitions.
- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary:* `#102342` background, white label, `8px` radius, hover state transitions to `#1E3A5F`.
  - *Secondary / Accent:* `#2563EB` background with white label for direct search actions.
  - *Outline / Ghost:* `1px solid #E2E8F0`, transparent background, `#102342` text; hover reveals `#F1F5F9`.
- **Citation Badges & Chips:**
  - Interactive micro-pills (`#EFF6FF` background, `#2563EB` text, `1px solid #DBEAFE`) inline with text. Hovering invokes a detailed metadata card showing author, publication year, journal title, and direct library call number.
- **Sidebar Navigation:**
  - Clean, minimal column featuring categorized history (Today, Previous 7 Days), bookmarked library catalogs (JSTOR, IEEE, University Archive), and an expandable user profile widget at the bottom.
- **Source Reference Cards:**
  - Embedded preview units below AI answers featuring paper title, author list, peer-reviewed indicator tag (`#0D9488`), and actionable "Locate in Stacks" or "Download PDF" links.
- **Form Controls & Radios:**
  - Focused inputs display a crisp dual-ring effect: `0 0 0 2px #FFFFFF, 0 0 0 4px #2563EB`.
